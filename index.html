Omnitrix 11-10: every new feature, with motion

Newest Studio captures from today. Videos loop and have no sound. Nothing here is installed yet; a preview build is being installed so you can try each one.

Alpha matte

Newest judge score: 8/10 (round 2)

How to try it in the plugin: Workspace/Inspector: select a mesh, pick Alpha matte and a map (crack, burn, swirl, streak, noise).

Real meshes get eaten away along a crack, burn, swirl, streak or noise map, with a glowing rim on every cut edge. The judge said the rim is one even pink band.

Camera shake v2

Newest judge score: 7.5/10 (round 2)

How to try it in the plugin: Plays when a hit or Moon camera marker fires; no new menu (replaces the old shake).

A smoother, softer camera shake that peaks fast and settles with no jump. The clip is the newest Studio replay of the shake (the whole view moves). Many quick hits still read as one long sway.

UI cleanup/scaling + animated anime themes

Newest judge score: 7.25/10 cleanup, 6.65/10 themes (round 3)

How to try it in the plugin: Workspace window > sidebar Themes > pick any of 15 anime themes (Animated UI toggle there). Resize any window from all 4 edges and corners; Ctrl = fine, Ctrl+Alt = finest, Shift = both sides.

Cleaner borders, resizing from every edge, and 15 animated anime themes. The clip cycles through the themes in Studio. Motifs are still subtle and theme textures are not uploaded yet.

Impact frames (snapshot to manga) + sub impact frame

Newest judge score: Speed 8, Strands 7.5, Smear 7, Sketch 6.5 (round 14)

How to try it in the plugin: Snapshot your scene in the Impact frames tool and pick a style (Speed, Strands, Smear, Sketch); the sub impact frame adds 3D rays.

Your scene is redrawn as black-and-white manga impact frames. The four clips are the day hero shot in each style, played from the Studio frames. Speed is the best so far.

Particle to 3D mesh in one click

Newest judge score: 7/10 (round 2, Slash to Card); pass 5 not judged

How to try it in the plugin: Select a ParticleEmitter, then use the Make mesh action in the Inspector.

Turns a particle into real 3D meshes that move the same way. Newest pass adds ring and dome shapes. Meshes still look bluer and dimmer than the glowing particle. Stills only, the lane has no motion frames yet.

Shaders tab

Newest judge score: 6.1/10 (round 2); newest self-check 3.5 to 6

How to try it in the plugin: Shaders tab (left nav, under Cutscene): pick a look on a selected rig or mesh; the eye icon is a live preview. Or Create > Shader.

Looks like fire scroll, hologram and burn dissolve that play on rigs and meshes. Looks are rough and six textures are not uploaded yet, so some draw plain.

Smoke Lab

Newest judge score: 6/10 (round 1 best); later rounds 4 to 5.5

How to try it in the plugin: Toolbar Bake > Bake Studio > CREATE > Smoke tab: pick a preset, Simulate, tweak, Bake flipbook 4x4 or 8x8.

Bakes cartoon smoke and fire sheets from a fluid simulation. No new Studio frames this hour, so these are the earlier sheets. Judges want flame tongues; the sim gives round blobs.

Slash lock (velocity-perpendicular fix)

Newest judge score: 8/10 Knight, 7.5 Kendo (pass 3)

How to try it in the plugin: Select a ParticleEmitter with Orientation VelocityPerpendicular, Inspector > Lock roll ON, rotate its attachment, Emit.

Keeps a slash card from spinning when you rotate its emitter. Native Studio sheets on Kendo, Knight and Qwuinkle; the lock holds in 15 poses. Spread 45 fans can still cross.

Surface Decals + Live Light Rig

Newest judge score: 7.5/10 best look (round 1, one judge)

How to try it in the plugin: Create > Surface decals (Getsuga burn line, Slam crack web, Scorch ring, Cursed energy splat, Ink splat), or select a ParticleEmitter > Inspector > Light rig > Add light rig.

Marks that wrap stairs and walls, plus lights that follow an effect. The daylight glow blow-out is fixed in the newest pass. The scorch ring has no true ring shape yet.

Anime Stepping + Inbetween Baker

Newest judge score: Baker 8 to 8.5/10 (round 1); cursed smoke 7

How to try it in the plugin: Anime stepping is an Inspector option on an effect (hold poses on twos); the Inbetween Baker is in Bake Studio.

Stepping holds poses like animation on twos. The clip is the newest Studio recording of a stepped red slash burst. The Baker makes smoother flipbook sheets from your own smoke, fire and wind.

Flow Fields & Swarms + Form & Shatter

Newest judge score: not scored yet (one early offline check: Sword assembly 7/10)

How to try it in the plugin: Create > Swarm presets (Sword assembly, Rune circle, Skull omen, Kanji in light, Bat cloud, Sand coffin and more).

Swarms of bits that gather into shapes like a sword or skull, then shatter. These loops are native Studio frames from the daytime run.

Body Emit + Arc Bake

Newest judge score: not scored yet

How to try it in the plugin: Select a rig and use Body Emit to put an aura on every limb; Arc Bake is design only.

Makes an aura come off every limb of a rig. The clip is the newest Studio recording of the cartoon flame and ink-flame auras on rigs. Blind comparison with the stock aura is still owed.

Scale tool

Newest judge score: not scored yet

How to try it in the plugin: Tools > Scale (or search scale): select an effect, a folder of effects or a character folder, drag the slider 0.1 to 3 (or type 0.6 / click .5 1 2 3), watch the preview, Apply (one Undo) or Cancel.

Resizes a whole effect or a folder of effects in one go, with a live preview and one Undo step. Character bodies stay full size, only their FX scale. No preview frames were captured yet.
