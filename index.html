Omnitrix overnight 11-10: best judged look per feature

Highest judge score first, not-yet-judged last. Click any picture to open it full size. Nothing here is installed yet.

Alpha matte

Best judge score: 8/10 (round 2)Saved, not installed (below 9)shakematte-1110 @ 9bee80a

Real meshes get eaten away along a crack, burn, swirl, streak or noise map, with a glowing rim on every cut edge. The judge liked how clearly each map reads, but said the rim is one even pink band and some views look like glowing lines drawn on the mesh, not a mesh being eaten.

Camera shake v2

Best judge score: 7.5/10 (round 2)Saved, not installed (below 9)shakematte-1110 @ 9bee80a

A smoother, softer camera shake that peaks fast and settles with no jump. The judge called the single heavy hit a clear win. His complaint: many quick hits in a row read as one long seasick sway, and the swing is not actually smaller than the old shake.

UI cleanup/scaling + animated anime themes

Best judge score: 7.25/10 (round 3) for cleanup; 6.65/10 for themesSaved, not installed (below 9)uithemes-1110 @ b29ec6d

Cleaner borders and resizing from every side and corner, plus 15 animated anime themes. Judges say the cleanup is still hard to see in full frames, and only about 4 of the 15 themes are recognisable without their name; the small moving dots read as dead pixels.

Impact frames (snapshot to manga) + sub impact frame

Best judge score: 7/10 (round 10 and earlier rounds)Saved, not installed (below 9)impactframes-1110 @ 1769961

Snapshot your scene and it is redrawn as black-and-white manga impact frames (Smear and Sketch styles shown), plus a 3D-rays sub impact frame. Best score is 7 for every style. Judges say the character is small and lumpy on wide shots and the ink lines look machine-made.

Particle to 3D mesh in one click

Best judge score: 7/10 (round 2, Slash to Card)Saved, not installed (below 9)particlemesh-1110 @ 946a10e

Turns a particle into real 3D meshes that move the same way. Shown: the slash effect as a 3D card and a shock cone, front and three-quarter views. Judges say the meshes look bluer and dimmer than the glowing particle, and cones show a hard cut at the open edge.

Shaders tab

Best judge score: 6.1/10 (round 2)Saved, not installed (below 9)shaders-1110 @ 492e946

A Shaders tab with looks like fire scroll, ghost flash and burn dissolve that play on rigs and meshes. Overall 6.1; the best single looks are Fire scroll (7.75) and Ghost flash (7.5). Judges say bright rigs wash out to pale slabs and Tornado burn reads like a plain texture scroll.

Smoke Lab

Best judge score: 6/10 (round 1, Explosion puff)Saved, not installed (below 9)smokelab-1110 @ 259af33

Bakes cartoon smoke and fire sheets from a fluid simulation. Best look is the Explosion puff at 6 in round 1 (later rounds slipped to 4-5.5). Judges want hand-drawn flame tongues and curled shapes; the simulation gives round blobs.
